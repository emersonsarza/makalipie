## Edited photography
Edit target for hero: B.Makali-Pie-2.jpg. Edit targets for the six product photos: existing small-pie images under public/images/brand/.
Supporting Christmas setting reference: Makalipie Christmas-14.jpg. Its product is never an insertion into other photos.

Prompt set for product edits:
BACKGROUND-ONLY photographic edit. First image is the actual product photograph; second is only a reference for subtle Christmas surroundings. Keep all foreground food and hands faithful in appearance and composition. Do not change ingredients, food shape, portions, quantity, crop or scale. Keep original food lighting, color, textures and photographic realism. Modify only distant non-food background: a softly defocused dark evergreen branch, small muted red ribbon or red gingham detail, and a few warm gold bokeh lights near the top outer edge, with much less decoration than the reference. Keep original plate, board and table, and target aspect ratio. Pie dominant and sharply detailed; props never cover or touch food or hands. No snow, text, logos, added garnish, illustrations or extra food. If space is tight, use very subtle blurred peripheral red/green only.

Per-product preservation instructions:
- Keylime: whipped cream swirl, lime zest, pale filling and crumb crust.
- Pecan: both partially cropped pecan pies and empty tart shell, nuts and crusts.
- Banoffee: cut-open banana tart, caramel, cream and fine chocolate shavings.
- S’mores: three toasted marshmallows, chocolate filling and fluted shortcrust.
- Oreo: both slices, their placement, crumble and white filling.
- Butter Chicken: both hands/fingers, opened flaky pie, chicken pieces and sauce.

Hero prompt: preserve the five original pies, their topping styles, quantities, proportions and warm wood/red gingham setting. Add only peripheral evergreen, restrained ribbon and softly defocused warm lights. No food decorations, extra food, text or graphics. A second landscape hero edit was reviewed but rejected because its keylime cream shape drifted from the source. The wide image received a fidelity correction using both the original spread and the authentic Keylime close-up: restore the rounded two-fold whipped-cream swirl and its open central curl; never use a solid rectangular slab. The corrected hero-wide.webp was reviewed and selected for its recognizable toppings and stronger responsive framing. The initial portrait edit is retained in this work folder. See comparison.jpg for the first edit and originals.
