## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: people in Cebu deciding whether to visit or order today, and people who already follow @makalipie and need a menu plus an order they can send. Both jobs are equal.

They are hungry, nearby, or planning a treat. They need to know what is baking, whether the kiosk or Sunday Market is open, how much it costs, and how to secure a pie without guessing.

## Product Purpose

Makalipie is a Cebuana tart-and-pie maker. The site exists so a first-time visitor and a returning Instagram customer can, in one sitting: believe the pies are real, see hours and place, and send a complete Instagram DM order.

Success this week: more complete DMs (people fill the form instead of asking what is available), more people finding Streetscape and the Sunday Market without DMing for directions, and the pies reading as Cebuana and handmade rather than a generic bakery template.

## Positioning

Handcrafted crust, proudly Cebuana-made in Banilad since 2020. Orders are confirmed person-to-person on Instagram — not a checkout cart. Buko is the bestseller and only sold Friday–Sunday via DM. Sundays add Butter Chicken Curry pie at the market.

A neighboring bakery site could not truthfully copy the Streetscape kiosk, the Buko window, the Sunday savory, or the Cebuano line “Makalipie gyud ni!”

## Operating Context

- Kiosk: 2nd Floor, Streetscape, Banilad, Cebu. Daily 10AM–8PM.
- Sunday Market: Butter Chicken Curry pie, Sundays ~7AM–3PM.
- Order path: fill the on-site form → copy summary → Instagram DM (@makalipie) → confirmation and invoice → pay (UnionBank, GCash, or cash) → pickup at the kiosk or Lalamove.
- Classic flavors are also findable on GrabFood; the site currently links a generic GrabFood search, not a store deep-link.
- Asia/Manila timezone for open/closed status.

## Product Principles

- Show the real pies and the real window (hours, Buko days, Sunday savory) before asking for a DM.
- Make a complete Instagram order the easy path; never pretend this is online checkout.
- Keep Cebuano warmth without inventing social proof.
- Walk-in and returning customers share one truth: what is baking, where, and how to secure it.
- Visual identity may change; names, prices, hours, place, payment, and the DM ritual may not.
