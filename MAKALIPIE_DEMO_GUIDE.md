# Makalipie Website Demo Guide

A simple walkthrough for showing Nika the website, side by side. Use test orders only.

---

## 1. Customer side (what customers see)

1. **Home page.** Show the banner at the top, the menu, "Our story", and "Find us". Any pop-up stops you've added show up here too.
2. **"Bring home a pie."** This button opens the order page.
   - *"This is where customers build their box."*
3. **Pick a branch and pies.** Tap + or − to choose how many. Some pies have extras, such as a message. Show the two order pages:
   - **Regular:** pies that are ready without extra days.
   - **Pre-order:** pies that need a few days to prepare. Earlier dates are blocked automatically.
4. **"Your little lineup."** The box on the side (or the bar at the bottom on a phone) shows what they picked and the total so far.
5. **Name, contact number, date, and time slot.** Only open days and one-hour slots appear.
6. **Pickup address.** It's shown on the form. Customers collect the order there, or book their own Grab or Lalamove to that address. We don't ask for their home address.
7. **Payment choice: GCash or Bank Transfer.** No money is paid on the website. Payment happens after you confirm.
8. **"Place order."** The pies are held for the customer for a short time (currently 30 minutes) while staff confirm.
9. **Their order page.** It shows:
   - The order number (for example MK-1234) with a **Copy number** button
   - **Open Instagram**, so they can paste the number in the chat
   - Their status, the date and time, the pickup address, and the order summary
   - It updates on its own as staff move the order along.
   - *"The customer keeps this page to follow their order."*
10. **If an order expired or wasn't approved,** the page offers two buttons under "Fancy another try?":
    - **Reopen:** puts the same order back in line, for the same day and time. This only works if nothing has changed: the pies are still on the menu, the price is the same, and there's still room that day.
    - **Reorder:** starts a new order with the same pies, so they can pick a new date.

## 2. Admin side (your back office)

The menu on the left shows these tabs in order. Staff accounts only see **Orders**, and only for their branch.

| Tab | What it's for | What to show | One line for Nika |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | A snapshot of today | Today's orders, what needs attention, how many to bake, payments, sales | "Your morning check: what's due today and what needs action." |
| **Catalog** | Your menu | Add a product with photo, description, and allergens. Under sizes and prices, set a fixed price or "DM for price", preparation days, and which days of the week it's sold. Block dates. Manage add-ons (extras). | "This is where you add pies, sizes, prices, and extras." |
| **Branches** | Each shop | The branch name, the **Pickup address** customers see, which sizes are sold there, and whether each one is **Regular** or **Pre-order** | "Choose what each branch sells and where customers pick up." |
| **Pop-ups** | Market or event stops | Add a listing with name, address, map link, dates, hours, and the items they'll find there. It shows on the website until its last date. | "Tell customers where you'll pop up next." |
| **Bakery schedule** | When each branch takes online orders | **Online / Paused** switch, first slot time, last slot cutoff, open weekdays, closed dates, and how many days ahead people can book | "Turn online ordering on or off, and set your hours and days off." |
| **Daily allocation** | How many of each pie you can take per day | A weekly grid per branch. **Blank = no limit. A number = the most you'll take that day. 0 = none that day.** It shows how many are already held. There's also **Today open / Today paused**, which stops new orders for today. | "Set how many of each pie you can make each day." |
| **Orders** | Every order | Search by name, order number, or branch. Filter by All / Awaiting / Active / Completed / Closed. Open an order to see the buttons for each step. | "Every order lands here, and you move it step by step." |

**Order statuses (in order):**
1. **Pending validation:** a new order. The pies are held for a short time. Button: *Start processing* (this stops the timer).
2. **Processing:** staff are checking it, usually while chatting on IG. Button: *Approve* or *Reject* (with a reason).
3. **Approved:** confirmed, waiting for payment. Button: *Record payment*.
4. **Preparing:** paid, now being made. The system won't allow this step until full payment is recorded. Button: *Ready for pickup*.
5. **Ready for pickup:** done and waiting. Button: *Complete pickup*.
6. **Completed:** picked up.
- **Expired:** nobody acted in time, so the hold ended.
- **Cancelled:** rejected by staff.
- Staff can also **Revise request** (change pies, date, or time) before it's completed. If the price changes, they record the extra payment or the refund.
