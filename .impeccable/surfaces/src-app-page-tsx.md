# Home / site

- **Scope:** Marketing site — home, menu, order — rebuilt as one mustard-flyer world.
- **Visitor mode:** Persuade on home; Operate on /menu and /order.
- **Audience:** Cebu walk-ins and returning Instagram customers, equally.
- **Job:** See what is baking, whether the window is open, and send a complete DM.
- **Constraints:** Facts in PRODUCT.md stay locked. No invented reviews, prices, or Grab deep-links. Unsplash stand-ins go. Visual identity is this direction, not the old cream site.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The site is the mustard flyer you can order from. It refuses the cream-serif bakery hero and the card-grid catalog.

**OWN-WORLD:** A drenched mustard sheet, charcoal ink, white oval price stickers, the scalloped smiley badge, condensed grotesque for names and a script only for “Makalipie gyud ni!”, real pie photography sitting on the yellow like die-cuts.

**STORY:** A visitor sees the real pies, a named hours threshold, and one action: send the Instagram order.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed mustard. Smiley badge top-right. The slogan as the display line. A live hours threshold across the sheet (Closed / Kiosk open / Buko window / Sunday Market), labeled in words. Three pie cutouts with oval prices. Stepped tabs Menu · Visit · Order, current tab as the yellow board. Primary control: copy the order path into Instagram.

**FORM:** Mustard flyer, alive — grounded candidate 5 of 7. Seed `75c3fc8d`. Signature interaction: the hours threshold, interruptible, bound to Asia/Manila.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
